About / 01

The part of AI that isn't the model.

I'm an AI engineer, currently an Associate Consultant at EY in Pune, building AI automation, LLM pipelines and agentic systems on Python and Azure. Around two years in the industry so far — most of it spent on the unfashionable half of applied AI: retrieval, evaluation, orchestration, and backends that stay standing when the model gets it wrong.

Before consulting I was an AI research intern at DRDO, teaching a humanoid robot to walk. Not metaphorically — a PPO policy in MuJoCo learning bipedal gait, stair ascent and descent, and curved paths from a reward function I spent months arguing with. It's still the clearest lesson I have about ML: the reward is the spec, and the spec is wrong the first time.

Since then the work has moved toward generative AI and agentic systems, but the instinct hasn't changed — build it, measure it honestly, keep the loop tight. Along the way I picked up a granted patent on real-time sentiment analysis and sentiment reversal for political news, and an IEEE paper on ML for stock market trend prediction. Right now I'm most interested in agents that plan, call tools, and recover when a step fails.
